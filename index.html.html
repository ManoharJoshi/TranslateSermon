Helper settingsసహాయకుడి సెట్టింగ్‌లు

1 · Gemini key కీ

Saved only on this phone and sent only to Google. Set a monthly spending limit on the key in Google AI Studio.

Set up another phone with a link
The link contains the key. Send it only privately (for example in a direct message to the person), never in a group or public post.

Opening the link on the other phone saves the key and these settings there, then removes the key from the address bar.

2 · Check everything అన్నీ పరీక్షించండి

Tests the key, translation, the Telugu voice, the listening service and the microphone. You will hear a short Telugu sentence.

    Tips
    
    
    • Use earphones or AirPods for the listener. With the phone speaker, the microphone also hears the Telugu voice, which can muffle the preacher and lose words.
    • With AirPods connected, choose the iPhone’s own microphone in section 5 — AirPods microphones are tuned to the wearer’s voice, not the pulpit.
    • Keep the screen on and the app open during the service — phones stop the microphone when locked.
    • Add it to the home screen: Share → “Add to Home Screen”.
